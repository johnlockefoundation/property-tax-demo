    HB 1089 · Levy Limit Demo
    How we calculated this
    What "levy limit" means
    A cap on how much the county's total property tax levy may grow each year.
    The assumed annual limit
    Inflation (CPI-U) plus the county's population growth.
    When the policy begins
    The 2019 base year; the limit compounds forward from there.
    What this receipt compares
    The five fiscal years FY2021-22 through FY2025-26: the county levy actually collected, summed, versus the summed limit.
    Taxes included
    County-wide property tax only, not the full bill.
    Property value
    The parcel's current assessed value from NC OneMap is held constant; reassessment timing is not modeled.
    Formula
    5-year actual rate = summed levy / assessed valuation; scenario levy = min(summed actual, summed 2019-base benchmark), so the limit never prescribes an increase.

    Sources: county levy data (Data(tax).csv), NCDOR LG04 FY2025-26 assessed valuation, NC OneMap parcels, U.S. Census population estimates, and BLS CPI-U.

    This is an estimate based on historical tax and property data and a hypothetical policy scenario. It is not an official tax assessment.
